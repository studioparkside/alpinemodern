About this page

My suggestion for the artwork and the color on every item in Round 2. Nothing here is designed yet. We go through it together, settle the open ones, and then I start.

ALPINE MODERN is spelled out and set on one line throughout, and the X stays small. Red, white and black are the ground, with the other colors showing up in application. The gridded pattern is the repeating graphic, and the coffee bags get a quiet abstract mountain. Items marked approved in Round 1 need nothing from you.
